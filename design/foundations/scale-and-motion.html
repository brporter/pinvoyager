Foundations

Space, radius, elevation, motion

The structural tokens. A 4px spacing base, six radii, four elevation levels, and four durations — small vocabularies on purpose, because a scale nobody can hold in their head gets ignored.

Steps 7, 9, 11, and 14 are deliberately absent. Gaps in a scale are what stop it from degenerating into "any multiple of four," which is the same as having no scale.

Elevation

Four levels, warm-tinted rather than pure black so shadows sit correctly on the warm neutral surfaces. In dark mode elevation is carried by surface lightness instead — shadow reads poorly against near-black, so the dark theme lifts surfaces as they come forward rather than deepening shadow.

--pv-shadow-1Cards at rest
--pv-shadow-2Hover, map controls
--pv-shadow-3Popovers, sheet
--pv-shadow-4Modals, lightbox

Motion

Hover any track below to play the transition.

--pv-duration-fast 140ms · ease-out — state changes, hovers
--pv-duration-base 220ms · ease-out — entrances, expansion
--pv-duration-slow 360ms · ease-sheet — the mobile photo sheet
--pv-ease-in-out — map pans, where both ends should settle
Reduced motion is handled at the token level. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, every duration token collapses to 1ms in tokens.css. Components inherit that automatically and must not re-implement the media query themselves — one place to get right instead of forty.

Map panning is the exception worth naming: MapLibre animates camera movement internally, outside CSS. Phase 7 has to pass the reduced-motion preference into the map's own animation options, or camera flights will keep animating for users who asked them not to.

Touch target minimum

44×44

Every interactive element gets at least a 44×44px hit area, even when its visible treatment is smaller. Map pins in particular look like 24px shapes but must accept a 44px tap — dense pin clusters are otherwise unusable on a phone.

Breakpoints

sm 480px · md 768px · lg 1024px · xl 1280px. These are documented as token values but must be written literally in media queries — custom properties are not valid in media conditions, and this trips people up regularly.
