Annotations

1The granularity of each voyage appears on its card. A browser scanning the grid can see that one creator publishes exact coordinates and another publishes to ~50 km, which sets expectations before the map even loads.
2Three columns on desktop, one on mobile. Two columns at 390px would put each cover photo at roughly 175px wide, which is too small for a photograph to do its job as the primary reason to click.
3The hero states the mechanism, not a slogan. "PinVoyager reads the location your camera recorded" tells a new visitor both what the product does and where the data comes from — which is also the thing they need to understand before uploading anything.
4The tab bar shows for signed-out visitors too, with Upload and Profile routing through sign-in. Hiding navigation from signed-out users makes the product look smaller than it is when every voyage is public anyway.
