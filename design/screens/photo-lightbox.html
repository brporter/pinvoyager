Annotations

1"shown to ~1 km" appears beside the coordinates. Displaying a rounded coordinate without saying it is rounded invites the reader to treat it as exact — which is precisely the misreading the granularity feature exists to prevent.
2"Show on map" closes the lightbox and selects the pin. The bidirectional selection from voyage detail continues to work from here rather than dead-ending.
3An unplaced photo says so plainly where the coordinates would be, rather than showing a blank field that reads as a loading failure.
4Camera make and model are absent. They are stripped from the served files per the D6 guarantee, so the interface has nothing to display — and showing them would contradict the promise made at upload.
5Keyboard: arrows navigate, Escape closes, focus is trapped inside the dialog and returns to the originating thumbnail on close. Phase 6 owns this; it is stated here so it does not get discovered late.
