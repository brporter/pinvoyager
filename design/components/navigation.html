The tab bar reserves env(safe-area-inset-bottom). On phones with a home indicator, a bar that stops at the viewport edge puts its controls underneath the system gesture area — taps get intercepted and the bottom row becomes unreliable. This is invisible in a desktop browser and obvious on hardware, which is why phase 8 requires device testing rather than a resized window.

Icon plus label on every tab. Icon-only tab bars force users to learn an arbitrary pictogram vocabulary, and "Discover" versus "Voyages" is not a distinction any icon pair conveys on its own.
