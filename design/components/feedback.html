Feedback and empty states

Badges, toasts, empty states, and skeletons. These are the components most often left until last and most often improvised — so they are specified here, and phase 6 implements them rather than reaching for a spinner.

Badges

Draft Public No location ~10 km 48 photos

The draft badge matters more than it looks. A voyage is world-readable the moment it is published, and the badge is the persistent reminder of which side of that line the current voyage sits on.

Toasts

Voyage published
“Three weeks in Hokkaido” is now visible to everyone.
2 photos could not be uploaded
One was not an image; one exceeded 25 MB.
Your session expired
Sign in again to finish uploading. Your queued photos are still here.
Toasts carry a left border in a status color, an icon, and a text title. Three signals, none of which is color alone. Success uses role="status" so it is announced politely; errors use role="alert" so they interrupt — a failed upload the user does not hear about is a failed upload they will not retry.

The session-expiry toast is the one worth studying. It happens mid-upload, and it tells the user their queued work survived. Without that sentence the honest assumption is that everything was lost, and the user starts over.

Empty states

No voyages yet — the user's own library

No voyages yet

A voyage is a set of photos on a map. Upload some pictures and PinVoyager will place them using the location data your camera recorded.

Unplaced photos — the no-GPS case

12 photos have no location

Screenshots, scans, and photos taken with location services turned off carry no GPS data. They are part of this voyage but do not appear on the map.

Nothing to discover

No public voyages yet

Be the first. Every voyage on PinVoyager is public.

Error — something failed to load

This voyage could not be loaded

Something went wrong on our end. Reference 7f3a-2b91 if you report it.

The error state shows a request ID and no internal detail. That pairing is deliberate: it gives a user something concrete to quote while keeping stack traces and internal messages on the server, where phase 2's error type logs the real cause.

Loading skeletons

Skeletons match the shape of what is coming, so the layout does not jump when content arrives. Under prefers-reduced-motion the shimmer stops and the skeleton becomes a flat block.
